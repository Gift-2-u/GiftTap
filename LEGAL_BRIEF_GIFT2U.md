## 2. Core loop (live + intended)

### Energy & daily max
- Play is gated by **daily energy / tap limits** (server-enforced max daily).
- Players cannot unlimited-farm in one day; they must wait for the next UTC day to continue.

### Free path
- Tap, refill over time, optional rewarded ads, levels, walls, streaks, boards, badges, referrals.
- No purchase required to play.

### Paid path — framed as **buying time / convenience** (not a bigger prize)
- **Premium boosts** and **NFTs** help players fill their daily energy or progress **faster**.
- Example: **Rush NFT** (e.g. common level 1) may add on the order of **+100 max daily energy** — more energy that day so they can **reach the same milestones sooner**, not a larger airdrop for that milestone.
- Other NFTs (Echo, Fate, Shadow, Locksmith, Star): multipliers, QoL, claims, walls — still subject to **daily caps** and durability where applicable.
- Operator position: NFTs/boosts = **time-buying** (hit the same goals with less calendar time), **not** “pay for a larger cash prize.”

### $G2U utility sinks
- Premium boosts, NFT durability reload, NFT level-ups, related shop use.

## 3. Proposed prize / airdrop redesign (compliance-oriented)

**Intent: no one “makes money out of competitive prizes.”**

| Feature | Proposed rule |
|---------|----------------|
| **Weekly / season leaderboards** | Still award **badges** (status / in-game). **No $G2U airdrop** from board rank. |
| **Mystery gift** | **Shards, free boosts, premium charges only** — no cash-out prize tokens from the box. |
| **$G2U airdrop** | **Individual milestones only** (e.g. clear L5, hit N taps). **Same milestone → same $G2U for every player.** No competition, no top-100 pot, no NFT-weighted bigger share for that milestone. |
| **NFT / boost effect on airdrop** | May help reach the milestone **faster** (more daily energy / speed). **Does not increase** the $G2U amount for that same milestone. |

### Equal free access (optional AMOE)
- Planned: free players may request the **same** boost/NFT by written request; same in-game effect as paid.
- Purchase = convenience; not the only way to obtain the item.

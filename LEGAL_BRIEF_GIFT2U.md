# Gift2U / Gift Tap — Product brief for legal counsel (Quebec)

**Purpose:** One-page overview for a Quebec lawyer (gaming / crypto-securities) and CPA.  
**Not legal advice.** Describes the product as built as of ~Sept 2026.  
**Operator intent:** Operate from Quebec, form a transparent Canadian company, monetize legally, comply with tax.

---

## 1. What it is

- **Gift Tap** — skill/time mobile & web game (tap-to-earn energy; optional walking / wall progression).
- **$G2U** — Solana SPL **in-game utility token** (game currency).
- **Distribution:** Website `gift2u.fun`, Solana Seeker / Android WebView shell, in-game wallet.
- **Token launch:** 1 September 2026 UTC (claims / premium $G2U shop gated to post-launch).

### Marketing / positioning (operator policy)
- **$G2U is marketed only as an in-game token** used inside Gift Tap (buy boosts, restore NFT durability, upgrade NFT levels, related shop utility).
- **Not** marketed as an investment, security, passive income, or “get rich / moon” product.
- Public messaging emphasizes **play / utility / game progression**, not price speculation.
- Operator will align ToS, site, and ads with this framing (counsel to confirm wording).

---

## 2. How players earn and spend

### Free path
- Tap with limited daily energy / battery; refill over time; optional rewarded ads (+daily capacity).
- Progress: levels, walls, streaks, weekly/season boards, badges, referrals.
- No purchase required to play the core loop.

### Paid / premium path (SOL and/or $G2U)
- **Timed boosts** (player chooses 1 / 3 / 7 days): e.g. +2K / +5K daily energy cap, 2× / 3× shard multipliers, expanded battery (500→1000).
- **Other shop:** bot, frenzy (short), once-per-day small boosts, mystery/crate-style items, badge burns.
- **NFTs (Solana):** Echo, Fate, Rush, Shadow, GiftLocksmith, Star — utilities (multipliers, daily caps, claims, wall perks). Mining NFTs have **durability** that drains with taps; reload with $G2U.
- **Project fee:** 0.0005 SOL to treasury on premium purchases.

### $G2U utility sinks (in-game)
- Buy premium boosts (post-launch).
- Reload NFT durability.
- Upgrade NFT levels.
- (Other shop / badge flows as configured.)

### $G2U sources (claimable allocations)
- Snapshot-based **airdrop_allocations** (L5 / weekly top-100 tier pots / monthly-season style).
- Players claim on-chain to their **game wallet** (user pays Solana network fees).
- Weekly **badges** are separate from weekly **$G2U** rows (badge grant ≠ airdrop amount).

## 3. Free vs paid / equal access (important for counsel)

- Free players can play and earn shards / progress / compete on boards without paying.
- Instant purchase (SOL / $G2U) is a **convenience** path for boosts and NFTs.
- **Planned equal free path (AMOE-style):** a free player may request the **same** premium boost or NFT by written request (e.g. mail letter describing which item they want); once granted, the item has the **same in-game effect** as if purchased.
- Intent: purchase is **not required** to obtain the same advantages; free and paid players can use the same items the same way.
- Counsel asked to confirm whether this equal-access design adequately addresses lottery / consideration concerns under Canadian law, and what process/disclosures are required to keep the free path genuine (timing, fulfillment, records).
